Common questions

  • Restore a purchase: Open Settings, choose Purchase or restore lifetime access, then choose Restore Purchases.
  • Move data: Export your inventory as CSV from Settings and import it on the other device.
  • Camera access: Open iOS Settings, select Asset Roll Call, and allow Camera access. Manual asset-code lookup remains available.
  • Delete data: Delete individual records in the app, use Delete All Local Data in Settings, or remove the app.

Privacy

Asset Roll Call works locally without an account, advertising, or a developer-operated backend. Optional privacy-limited usage analytics is off until you choose to share it and can be disabled in Settings.
